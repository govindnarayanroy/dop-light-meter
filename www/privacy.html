DoP Meter — Privacy Policy

Effective: July 14, 2026

The short version

DoP Meter does not collect, store, or transmit any data. Period.

Camera

DoP Meter uses your device camera to measure lighting — contrast ratios, color temperature, and exposure. All image processing (including face detection) happens entirely on your device. No frames, images, or measurements ever leave your device. Nothing is saved to your photo library or anywhere else.

Data collection

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts, and makes no network requests. The face-detection model is bundled inside the app and runs offline.
